# Track B — Provider outreach posture

## Positioning
Approach providers as a fellow seller learning the same market, not as an outside analyst asking them to validate SHSY.

Until SHSY has real paid results, say this plainly:
- we are putting our own paid agent endpoint into the market;
- we do not yet have a meaningful benchmark/result to claim;
- we are encountering the same uncertainty around discovery, qualified demand, buyer reach, repeat use and pricing;
- if they have learned a better way, we would genuinely value their advice.

After SHSY has real external buyer results, replace the abstract statement with the observed result and compare the problem, not the provider's confidential data.

## Message sequence
1. Show one specific fact proving we looked at their paid agent product.
2. State the shared problem: “we're selling into the same agent-payment market and are trying to understand X too.”
3. Admit current evidence boundary; never imply a benchmark/data asset we do not have.
4. Ask what has actually been hard for them and what they learned.
5. Ask for advice before asking for data, installation, telemetry or a pilot.
6. If a concrete decision-changing metric emerges, follow up with the smallest possible exchange/spec.

## Default pre-results language
“We’re starting to sell to agents ourselves rather than study the market only from the outside. We don’t have a benchmark worth claiming yet. We’re running into the same question around [their pain]. If you’ve found a better way to think about it, I’d genuinely value the advice. What has been the hardest part on your side?”

## Default post-results language
“We’ve now seen [real observed SHSY result] on our own paid endpoint. It made your earlier point about [pain] much more concrete for us. We’re still not claiming it generalizes. I’d like to compare notes on what would actually distinguish a pricing problem from a demand/discovery problem without asking either side to expose raw buyer data.”

## Do not
- lead with “pricing network”;
- claim proprietary market data before it exists;
- ask for raw rows/wallet identities;
- ask them to install third-party code in the first interaction;
- send a generic survey;
- pretend SHSY has solved a problem we are still experiencing ourselves.
