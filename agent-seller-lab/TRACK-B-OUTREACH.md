# Track B — Provider outreach posture

## Positioning
Approach providers as a fellow seller learning the same market, not as an outside analyst asking them to validate SHSY.

Until SHSY has real paid results, say this plainly:
- we are putting our own paid agent endpoint into the market;
- we do not yet have a meaningful benchmark/result to claim;
- we are encountering the same uncertainty around discovery, qualified demand, buyer reach, repeat use and pricing;
- if they have learned a better way, we would genuinely value their advice;
- make the relationship reciprocal: if they share useful experience/insight with us, we will share back material, non-confidential insights we learn from our own seller experiment and broader market conversations when we have something genuinely useful.

Do not promise confidential information from other providers, raw data, or a guaranteed future benchmark. The exchange is insight-for-insight, subject to confidentiality and aggregation boundaries.

After SHSY has real external buyer results, replace the abstract statement with the observed result and compare the problem, not the provider's confidential data.

## Message sequence
1. Show one specific fact proving we looked at their paid agent product.
2. State the shared problem: “we're selling into the same agent-payment market and are trying to understand X too.”
3. Admit current evidence boundary; never imply a benchmark/data asset we do not have.
4. Ask what has actually been hard for them and what they learned.
5. Offer reciprocity: useful insights they share will be met with useful non-confidential insights we learn, when we have them.
6. Ask for advice before asking for data, installation, telemetry or a pilot.
7. If a concrete decision-changing metric emerges, follow up with the smallest possible exchange/spec.

## Default pre-results language
“We’re starting to sell to agents ourselves rather than study the market only from the outside. We don’t have a benchmark worth claiming yet, and we’re running into the same question around [their pain]. You’ve been doing this longer than we have, so if you’ve found a better way to think about it, I’d genuinely value the advice. And if you’re willing to share what you’ve learned, I’ll reciprocate: as we get real results from our own endpoint and learn things that are genuinely useful and non-confidential, I’ll send those back rather than make this a one-way research request. What has been the hardest part on your side?”

## Default post-results language
“We’ve now seen [real observed SHSY result] on our own paid endpoint. It made your earlier point about [pain] much more concrete for us. We’re still not claiming it generalizes. You were helpful to us earlier, so I wanted to send the observation back. I’d like to keep comparing notes on what would actually distinguish a pricing problem from a demand/discovery problem without asking either side to expose raw buyer data.”

## Do not
- lead with “pricing network”;
- claim proprietary market data before it exists;
- ask for raw rows/wallet identities;
- ask them to install third-party code in the first interaction;
- send a generic survey;
- pretend SHSY has solved a problem we are still experiencing ourselves;
- imply we will redistribute another provider's confidential insight to others.
