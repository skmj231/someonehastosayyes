Start safely.Send your first 10 articles to approval regardless of score. Once you have seen real results, turn on the automatic publish path using thresholds that fit your content.
A

Check and ask

  1. Trigger when an article is ready for review

    Use your existing article source. WordPress, Webflow, Google Docs, Airtable, and any CMS trigger will work. Trigger on a draft or “Ready for review” status, never on an already published article.

  2. Add Winston AI: Detect AI Content

    Map the full article body into Text. Choose the latest model. Winston recommends long-form text; use at least 300 words and preferably the complete article.

    Text = Article body

  3. Add Winston AI: Detect Plagiarism

    Map the same article body into Text. Test both Winston steps once so Zapier exposes the returned scores as fields.

    Text = Article body

  4. Save one row in Google Sheets

    Create these columns once, then map the article and Winston results.

    titledraft_urlhuman_scoreplagiarismdecisionstatus
    Article titleCMS draft URLWinston scoreWinston resultblankchecked

  5. Add Paths by Zapier

    Use Winston’s overall Human Score as a routing signal, not a verdict. A practical starting point after calibration is Human Score 80 or higher plus your own acceptable plagiarism limit.

    Path 1: Ready

    Both checks meet your thresholds. Update the sheet to “ready.” You may publish automatically after the first 10 supervised runs.

    Path 2: Review

    Use the fallback path for every other result. Add Webhooks by Zapier, choose Custom Request, and send the approval request below.

  6. Copy this request into the Review path

    Method is POST. URL is https://someonehastosayyes.com/v1/approvals. Add the two headers, then paste the JSON body. Map the fields shown in braces from earlier Zap steps.

    HEADERS
    Authorization: Bearer YOUR_SHSY_API_KEY
Content-Type: application/json
Idempotency-Key: {{Article ID}}
    JSON BODY
    {
  "question": "Publish {{Article Title}}?",
  "context": {
    "action": "content.publish",
    "title": "{{Article Title}}",
    "draft_url": "{{Draft URL}}",
    "human_score": "{{Winston Human Score}}",
    "plagiarism_result": "{{Winston Plagiarism Result}}"
  },
  "approve_label": "Publish",
  "reject_label": "Needs revision",
  "channel": "email",
  "to": "YOUR_APPROVER_EMAIL",
  "callback_url": "YOUR_ZAP_B_CATCH_HOOK_URL",
  "timeout_minutes": 1440,
  "default_on_timeout": "rejected"
}

    Send only the title, draft link, and scores. Do not send the full article to the approval service.

B

Receive the answer and publish

  1. Create the Catch Hook first

    In a new Zap, choose Webhooks by Zapier → Catch Hook. Copy its URL and place it in Zap A as callback_url.

  2. Test one approval

    Run Zap A once, open the approval email, and choose Publish. Return to Zap B and test the trigger. Zapier should show approved: true, the decision, and the approval ID.

  3. Add two Paths

    Approved is true

    Publish the existing draft in your CMS. Then update the Google Sheets row to “published.”

    Fallback

    Keep the article as a draft. Update the row to “needs revision” and notify the writer.

  4. Test yes, no, and no answer

    Confirm that only the approved path can publish. A rejection or 24-hour timeout must leave the article unpublished.

One article is enough to test the whole path.
